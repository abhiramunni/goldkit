Should I buy now?

For a wedding, yes — for a punt, nobody knows.

Gold is at a 14-month low after falling almost 15% in a month. If you are buying for a wedding or a festival, a dip is when the metal costs least. If you are buying because you think it will bounce, remember: nobody can time the bottom.

Why it fell, in plain words:

  • A hawkish US Fed — rate-cut hopes faded, so gold lost shine.
  • A strong dollar — gold gets dearer in rupees when the dollar rises.
  • High US Treasury yields — bonds pay more, so some money left gold.

This is context, not investment advice. Buy for the occasion, in a budget you fixed first.

The bill, before the bill

What will my jewellery actually cost?

Making charges are where the money goes
Plain gold: 8–12% is normal. Heavy design work: up to 20–25%. Above 25% — walk away and compare.
1–3% is normal for machine-made pieces. Handmade filigree can be more — ask them to show it on the bill.

GST is 3% on the metal value and 5% on the making charges — the standard split jewellers use.

Before you pay

Hallmark, purity and the HUID number

Every piece of gold jewellery sold in India must carry a BIS hallmark. Three marks, one look — check them before the bill is printed.

Every hallmarked piece has a unique HUID code stamped on it. Check it on the BIS Care app before you pay — it confirms the jeweller, the purity and that the hallmark is genuine.
